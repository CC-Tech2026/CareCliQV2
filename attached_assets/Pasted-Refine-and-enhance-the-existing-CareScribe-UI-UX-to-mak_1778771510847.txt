Refine and enhance the existing CareScribe UI/UX to make the platform feel more polished, user-friendly, modern, and visually cohesive while maintaining the current functionality and system structure.

visual consistency
spacing and alignment
readability
responsiveness
user friendliness
modern healthcare SaaS polish

professional
trustworthy
calm
approachable
operationally efficient

overly corporate
overly technical
cluttered
AI-generated
cold or harsh

soft neutral backgrounds
subtle gradients where appropriate
rounded cards and components
gentle shadows
modern spacing
clean typography
soft accent colors (periwinkle, blush, lavender, sage, muted pinks)

better text visibility and contrast

more breathing room between components
consistent button styling
consistent card layouts
aligned spacing throughout pages
cleaner tables and forms
improved empty states
