BUSINESS MODEL
==============

CareScribe has 3 platform roles:

1. Support Coordinator / Organization Admin

* Owns the provider organization
* Can manage workers
* Can view all organization participants
* Can access compliance, billing, reports, incidents
* Can invite staff

2. Support Worker

* Can ONLY access allocated participants
* Can ONLY access their own sessions/incidents
* Cannot access billing/admin areas
* Cannot view other workers' notes

3. Allied Health Professional

* Own caseload only
* Clinical reporting access
* Body map + therapy reporting
* Independent invoicing access
